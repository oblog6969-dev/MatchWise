### 🧩 18 Clinically Grounded Major Segments (`traits.js`)
Deconstructed all 6 tiers of Maslow's hierarchy into 3 granular psychological pillars each (18 total), with dynamic scoring ($0\% - 100\%$) derived from OCEAN traits, emotional intelligence, attachment coordinates, differentiation of self, and Kegan stages:
1. **✨ Self-Transcendence (Apex)**:
   - *Transpersonal Mission & Generational Legacy* (`transpersonal_mission`)
   - *Altruism & Generative Compassion* (`altruistic_service`)
   - *Spiritual Unity & Sacred Meaning* (`spiritual_unity`)
2. **🌟 Self-Actualization**:
   - *Authenticity & Core Values Alignment* (`authentic_alignment`)
   - *Creative Potential & Intellectual Expansion* (`creative_growth`)
   - *Personal Sovereignty & Autonomous Freedom* (`personal_sovereignty`)
3. **🏆 Esteem, Mastery & Respect**:
   - *Self-Worth, Dignity & Inner Sovereignty* (`self_worth_dignity`)
   - *Competence, Mastery & Achievement* (`competence_mastery`)
   - *Mutual Admiration & Partner Validation* (`mutual_admiration`)
4. **💖 Love, Belonging & Intimacy**:
   - *Deep Emotional Intimacy & Attunement* (`emotional_intimacy`)
   - *Unconditional Acceptance & Tender Warmth* (`unconditional_acceptance`)
   - *Companionship & Connection Rituals* (`shared_rituals`)
5. **🛡️ Safety, Security & Predictability**:
   - *Financial Predictability & Resource Prudence* (`financial_predictability`)
   - *Emotional Safety & Non-Threatening Space* (`emotional_safety`)
   - *Domestic Order & External Boundary Clarity* (`domestic_order`)
6. **🌿 Somatic Homeostasis & Rhythm**:
   - *Rest, Sleep & Somatic Recovery* (`rest_recovery`)
   - *Nervous System Grounding & De-escalation* (`nervous_regulation`)
   - *Vitality, Pacing & Sensory Ease* (`vitality_rhythm`)

Each segment includes complete bilingual definitions, relational impact dynamics, and actionable couple practices.

### 🎨 Creative Tri-Perspective Visualizer (`script.js`, `style.css`, `utils.js`)
- **Tri-Perspective View Switcher**:
  - `[ 🏛️ Pyramid Spectrum ]`: Redesigned SVG pyramid featuring mini segment cells inside each tier, widened apex layout eliminating text overlap, side-anchored partner share badges, and interactive tooltips.
  - `[ 🧩 Segment Matrix (18 Pillars) ]`: Comprehensive side-by-side comparative dashboard displaying all 18 segments with dual partner meters and synergy classifications.
  - `[ 💡 Dyadic Growth Compass ]`: Practical relationship blueprint offering tailored instructions to nourish each partner's primary need, along with weekly check-in synthesis accords.
- **Dynamic Segment Inspector Panel**:
  - Interactive tier selector chips and detailed segment cards displaying:
    - **Category Indicators**: D-Needs (Deficiency) vs. B-Needs (Growth/Being).
    - **Dyadic Synergy Badges**: *Synergistic Alignment*, *Complementary Balance*, or *Active Growth Area*.
    - **Dual Comparative Progress Bars**: Green (Partner A) and Amber (Partner B) score meters.
    - **Actionable Couple Practices**: Specific behavioral exercises to cultivate intimacy and mutual respect.
- **Full Bilingual RTL Parity**: Seamless right-to-left layout and localized terminology for both English and Arabic.
