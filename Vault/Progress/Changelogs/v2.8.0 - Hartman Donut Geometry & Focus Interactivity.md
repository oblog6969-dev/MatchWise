# 🎨 Release v2.8.0 — Hartman Donut Geometry & Dynamic Focus

> [!summary] Release Details
> **Version:** `v2.8.0`  
> **Release Date:** September 2026  
> **Milestone:** [[M12 - Hartman Circle Chart & Dynamic Focus]]  
> **Status:** ✅ Production Ready

---

### ✨ Added
- **Exact Trigonometric SVG Arc Geometry (`describeDonutSlice`)**: Replaced deprecated `stroke-dashoffset` circle calculations with exact SVG `<path d="M ... A ... L ... A ... Z">` math, eliminating Chromium `transform-origin` translation artifacts.
- **Direct Percentage Badges on Slices**: Added embedded bold percentage labels placed along radial mid-angles with intelligent luminance fill colors.
- **Dynamic Interactive Focus & Shadowing**:
  - Interactive person filter buttons (`[Tariq Al-Mansoor]`, `[Nour Al-Sabah]`, `[Both / كلاهما]`).
  - Clicking a person highlights their ring while gracefully shadowing the partner's ring to 14% opacity.
  - Center hub dynamically spotlighting the active partner's dominant motive and core drive.
- **Structured Comparison Micro-Rows**:
  - Clean 3-column micro-rows (`[Name]` `[Bar]` `[Percentage]`) replacing verbose multi-line cards.

### 🐛 Fixed
- **Scope Resolution (`nameB`)**: Hoisted `nameB` to top of `generateAndRenderReport()` scope, eliminating runtime ReferenceError.
- **Variable Hoisting in Comparison Mode**: Resolved block-scoping in `renderHartmanDonut()`, ensuring `applyFocus("B")` updates the center hub cleanly without console warnings.
