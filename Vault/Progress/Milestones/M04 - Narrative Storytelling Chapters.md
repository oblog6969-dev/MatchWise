## 🎯 Objective
Transform dry psychometric percentages into an emotionally resonant, clinically structured narrative dossier organized as **5 Individual Chapters** and **4 Dyadic Acts**.

---

## 🔑 Narrative Architecture

### The 5 Individual Chapters
1. **Chapter 1: The Core Self & Motivations** — MBTI Type, Hartman Color Motive, DISC Profile, and Adult Attachment Style.
2. **Chapter 2: Submerged Needs & Stress Triggers** — The Birkman Iceberg (Usual vs. Hidden Needs vs. Stress Reactivity).
3. **Chapter 3: Interpersonal Exchange & Reciprocity** — FIRO-B dimensions (Affection, Control, Inclusion) and Schwartz Values.
4. **Chapter 4: Conflict Mechanics & De-escalation** — TKI Conflict Modes and Gottman Four Horsemen Vulnerability index.
5. **Chapter 5: Evolutionary Growth & Operating Manual** — Concrete dos & don'ts for interacting with this profile.

### The 4 Dyadic Acts (Relationship Comparison)
1. **Act I: The Chemistry of Temperaments** — Harmony and friction of motives and cognitive styles.
2. **Act II: Hidden Vulnerabilities & Attachment Dance** — Anxiety vs. Avoidance collision points and safety needs.
3. **Act III: The Conflict Cycle & Repair Mechanics** — Escalation loop simulation and actionable repair interventions.
4. **Act IV: The Long-Term Horizon & Shared Mission** — Alignment on foundational values, life pacing, and mutual growth.
